Why sending your price early kills 90% of DM sales 💸

Hey {{ name }}, yesterday you claimed The 15-Minute DM Objection Matrix. Today, I want to address the single biggest mistake service providers and creators make in direct messages:

The Mistake: Replying to "How much is this?" with a raw price tag in your very first response.

When you send a price before establishing perceived value, the prospect's brain immediately compares your price tag against their current bank balance — rather than the problem you are solving for them.

The 3-Step Value Anchor Framework:

  1. Acknowledge & Re-frame: Validate their question, but ask 1 clarifying question about their business goal first.
  2. Anchor Expected ROI: Highlight the result (e.g. 5 new high-paying clients) before stating cost.
  3. Offer Choice, Not Ultimatum: Present a clear tier structure so they choose between options rather than saying yes/no.
