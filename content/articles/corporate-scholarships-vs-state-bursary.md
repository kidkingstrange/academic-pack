# Private Corporate Scholarships vs Government Bursaries

You need money for university.

You hear coursemates discussing two different educational funding opportunities on campus:

On one side: **Private Corporate Scholarships** (NNPC/Shell, Chevron JV, MTN Foundation, TotalEnergies).

On the other side: **Government & State Bursaries** (Delta State Bursary, Lagos State Scholarship Board, Kano State Educational Grants).

Both offer non-repayable cash that can transform your student life. 

Yet, their application requirements, competition intensity, verification processes, and payout sums could not be more different.

Should you invest your energy preparing for brutal corporate aptitude tests, or should you focus on claiming your statutory state indigene welfare grants?

Here is the definitive comparative breakdown of **Corporate Scholarships vs. Government Bursaries in Nigeria**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which pays more money: private corporate scholarships or government bursaries?
Private corporate scholarships (Shell, Chevron, MTN, TotalEnergies) pay significantly higher amounts (₦150,000 to ₦300,000+ annually) compared to state government bursaries (typically ₦20,000 to ₦50,000 annually).

### Can a student hold both a corporate scholarship and a state government bursary simultaneously?
Yes. Most corporate scholarship policies prohibit holding two simultaneous corporate awards (e.g., holding Shell and Chevron together), but permit students to collect statutory state indigenous bursaries.

### What is the primary selection criteria for corporate scholarships vs bursaries?
Corporate scholarships are strictly merit-driven, requiring a high CGPA (3.50+), aptitude test scoring, and verification. State bursaries are non-competitive welfare grants awarded to verified indigenes of specific regions.

### How can an average student win educational funding if their CGPA is below 3.50?
Focus on state indigenous bursaries, local government educational grants, community town union scholarships, and federal loan facilities like NELFUND which do not enforce high CGPA cutoff barriers.
