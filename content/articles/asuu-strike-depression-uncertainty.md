# Strike Depression: Coping When Your Life Is Put on Pause

The official announcement flashes on your phone screen:

*"The Academic Staff Union of Universities (ASUU) has declared an indefinite, comprehensive, and total strike action with immediate effect."*

You pack your bags, vacate your hostel room, and board a commercial bus back to your parents' house.

At first, you tell yourself it will only last a few weeks. But as week 4 turns into month 4, and month 4 stretches into month 8, an insidious psychological poison begins to seep into your daily life.

You watch peers from private universities (Babcock, Covenant, Bowen) celebrate their convocation and National Youth Service Corps (NYSC) postings on LinkedIn. You watch former secondary school classmates living abroad graduate on time.

Meanwhile, you are trapped in your childhood bedroom, answering intrusive questions from relatives: *"So which level are you now? When are you people finishing?"*

You feel cheated. You feel stagnant. You feel deeply, numbly depressed.

You are experiencing **Strike-Induced Existential Depression**.

---

## 1. The Psychology of Suspended Animation

In clinical psychology, indefinite strikes inflict trauma through **Ambiguous Loss and Suspended Life Momentum**:

When a student experiences a defined setback (such as repeating a class), the timeline is clear: you repeat for 9 months, then move forward.

During an ASUU or state university strike:
* You cannot commit to a corporate 6-month internship because the strike could end next Monday.
* You cannot study intensively because exams feel distant and abstract.
* You cannot fully relax because the threat of sudden resumption hangs over your head.

Living in chronic limbo paralyses long-term executive planning, triggering profound existential dread and learned helplessness.

## 2. The 3 Dangerous Traps of the Strikebound Student

To protect your mental health during prolonged campus shutdowns, avoid these three psychological traps:

### Trap 1: The "Waiting Room" Fallacy
Believing that your real life only resumes when the university registrar signs a reopening memo. Your life is not on pause; only your degree registration is paused. Every month spent waiting idly is an irreplaceable month of your youth burned away.

### Trap 2: The Social Comparison Spiral
Endlessly refreshing Instagram and LinkedIn to monitor peers who are graduating or landing high-paying jobs. Remember: academic timelines in public tertiary institutions are dictated by institutional politics, not personal merit.

### Trap 3: Total Cognitive Atrophy
Abandoning intellectual rigor entirely for binge-watching Netflix or mindless social media browsing. When resumption suddenly occurs, your de-conditioned brain will suffer severe cognitive resistance.

## 3. The Counter-Offensive: The Self-Directed Fellowship Protocol

Transform an institutional disaster into an unfair competitive advantage:

1. **Adopt a Secondary Skill Track:** Dedicate 4 hours daily to mastering an income-generating digital or practical craft: software engineering, UI/UX design, performance copywriting, video editing, or data analytics.
2. **Build an Independent Project:** Do not wait for a final-year supervisor to give you permission to research. Build a portfolio, write case studies, or launch a micro-enterprise.
3. **Protect Your Peer Network:** Stay connected with serious departmental coursemates. Create a weekly study group or mastermind call to discuss industry trends and keep each other motivated.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is 'Strike Depression' among Nigerian university undergraduates?
A distinct form of situational existential depression triggered by indefinite academic strikes. Students experience helplessness, anger at institutional betrayal, loss of identity, and grief over delayed graduation milestones.

### How does prolonged academic uncertainty impact student mental health?
Inability to plan for the future. When students cannot predict whether exams resume in two weeks or eight months, chronic cortisol elevation produces apathy, sleep disturbances, and loss of ambition.

### What is the single most effective psychological strategy during a prolonged university shutdown?
Cognitive de-centering: mentally decoupling your personal development and identity from the university calendar. Treat the strike as a self-directed fellowship to build real-world digital, technical, or trade skills.

### How can students avoid completely forgetting their coursework during strikes?
Execute a light 'Micro-Maintenance' routine: review high-yield course summaries for 30 minutes twice a week to keep neural memory traces active without burning out.
