# Handling Lodge Burglary: Immediate Recovery Steps

You arrive back at your student lodge at 4:30 PM after writing your afternoon examination. 

You walk down the tiled corridor, humming quietly, keys in hand. 

As you reach Room 6, you freeze.

Your wooden door is slightly ajar. 

The brass padlock latch has been wrenched out of the door frame, leaving splintered wood scattered across the floor tiles. 

You push the door open with trembling fingers. 

Inside, your room has been turned inside out:
* Wardrobe doors are flung open; your clothes are dumped in a heap on the floor.
* Your study desk is bare: your HP laptop, your laptop charger, and your external hard drive containing four years of project research are gone.
* Your backup smartphone and 30,000mAh power bank have vanished.
* Even the ₦15,000 cash in your drawer has been taken.

A cold wave of panic, nausea, and shock washes over your entire body. Your knees feel weak.

Burglary is every student’s worst nightmare. In minutes, months of academic work, expensive electronic devices, and personal security are erased.

What you do in the next sixty minutes can mean the difference between recovering your assets or having your bank accounts emptied by criminals.

Here is the exact **24-Hour Emergency Response Protocol** following a student lodge burglary.

---

## 1. The Immediate 60-Minute Emergency Sequence



**The 60-Minute Post-Burglary Protocol:**
* Step 1: Digital Quarantine (Block stolen SIMs & USSD bank mobile channels) — Step 2: Crime Scene Preservation (Do NOT touch surfaces or sweep rooms) — Step 3: Formal Police Incident Report (Obtain official Crime Extract Document) — Step 4: Academic Contingency (Notify Departmental HOD & Course Rep)



---

### Step 1: Immediate Digital Quarantine (Prevent Financial Plunder)
Most thieves do not just sell stolen phones—they immediately swap the SIM card into another phone to intercept OTPs and drain bank accounts via USSD codes (*737#, *901#, *894#).

Execute this sequence within twenty minutes:
1. **Dial Bank Deactivation Codes:** Borrow a neighbor's phone and dial your bank's instant USSD freeze code (e.g., GTBank: `*737*51*10#`, Zenith: `*966*911#`, Access: `*901*911#`).
2. **Call Your Telco Provider:** Contact MTN, Airtel, or Glo customer support to deactivate your stolen SIM cards immediately.
3. **Remote-Wipe Gadgets:** Log into Google *Find My Device* (for Android) or Apple *Find My* (for iPhone/Mac) from a browser. Transmit a remote lock and wipe command to protect your personal photos and passwords.

---

## 2. Step 2: Crime Scene Preservation & Police Documentation

Never clean your room or sweep away debris before law enforcement inspects the scene:
* **Preserve Fingerprints:** Intruders often leave clear fingerprints on door handles, drawer edges, and window burglary bars.
* **Obtain the Official Police Crime Extract:** Go to the nearest Divisional Police Headquarters (DPO). File a formal complaint detailing the date, time, and exact serial numbers/IMEIs of stolen items. Insist on collecting an authenticated **Police Extract and Incident Report**. 
* You will need this official police extract to block your SIM cards, replace your student ID card, and legally justify missed academic tests.

---

## 3. Step 3: Departmental Notification for Lost Academic Work

If your laptop contained your only copy of your final-year research project, term papers, or lab data:
* Immediately write a formal letter through your Academic Adviser to your Head of Department (HOD), attaching a copy of the police report.
* Request a 7 to 14-day extension for assignment submission or defense preparation.
* In the future, always automate cloud backups using Google Drive, OneDrive, or GitHub to ensure hardware loss does not destroy your academic degree.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the very first thing you should do after discovering your room was burgled?
Do not enter or touch anything in the room. Touching surfaces contaminates fingerprint and forensic evidence. Immediately alert your neighbors, lodge caretaker, and call the local police divisional headquarters.

### How can you prevent thieves from draining your bank accounts after stealing your phone?
Call your mobile network provider immediately from a friend's phone to block your stolen SIM card, and dial your bank's emergency USSD deactivation code to freeze all mobile banking transactions.

### Will the local police recover your stolen laptop or phone?
Recovery rates vary. Providing police with the unique 15-digit IMEI number (for phones) or serial number/MAC address (for laptops) enables tracking via cellular network blacklists and technical intelligence units.

### How should a student cope with the psychological trauma after a break-in?
Experiencing hyper-vigilance, insomnia, and fear after a burglary is normal acute stress. Temporarily relocate to a friend's room, speak with campus counselors, and harden physical locks before sleeping alone again.
