# Renewing Current Lodge vs Moving to New Compound: Signs

As the academic session draws to a close, a familiar white slip of paper slips under your lodge door:

*"Notice of Rent Review and Renewal for the Upcoming Academic Session."*

You open it to discover that your landlord has increased your rent by **₦80,000**, added a new *"Compound Maintenance Fee"*, and expects full payment within 14 days of resumption.

Instantly, the debate ignites:
* Should you pay the increased rent and renew your lease?
* Or should you pack your boxes, hire a logistics mini-truck, and move to a new compound?

Moving is exhausting, expensive, and risky. But staying in an unlivable compound destroys your academic peace. Here is the strategic 5-point audit to decide whether to renew or relocate.

---

## 1. The Moving Sunk-Cost Audit: Calculate the "Exit Tax"

Undergraduates often assume moving to a lodge priced ₦40,000 cheaper will save money. They forget the **relocation friction costs**:

* **New Agent Registration & Inspection Fee:** ₦5,000 – ₦10,000
* **New Agent Commission (10%):** ₦30,000 – ₦45,000
* **New Legal/Agreement Fee (10%):** ₦30,000 – ₦45,000
* **New Caution Deposit:** ₦25,000 – ₦40,000
* **Logistics Truck / Keke Moving Trips:** ₦15,000 – ₦25,000
* **Room Cleaning, Minor Plumbing, and Repainting:** ₦15,000
* **Total Moving Friction Expense:** **₦120,000 – ₦180,000**.

If your current landlord's rent increment is ₦50,000, paying the increment is **₦70,000 cheaper** than moving to a new lodge with identical base rent.

Only move if the new compound delivers massive functional improvements that justify the moving expense.

## 2. The 4 Non-Negotiable Signs You MUST Move Out

There are situations where paying any moving fee is infinitely better than staying:

1. **Security Vulnerability:** If your compound was burgled during the session and the landlord refused to install security razor wire, iron gate deadbolts, or hire night guards, your life is in danger. Move immediately.
2. **Permanent Water Collapse:** If the borehole pump has been dead for two months and you spend 90 minutes hauling buckets from neighbouring wells before every 8:00 AM class, your academic stamina is being depleted.
3. **Chronic Toxic Neighbors:** If adjacent rooms have been rented to disruptive non-students, constant partygoers, or hostile individuals who threaten your safety, no degree of quiet study is possible.
4. **Predatory Landlord Retaliation:** If the caretaker locks compound water taps or cuts electricity lines whenever tenants raise legitimate maintenance grievances, the relationship is irrecoverable.

Review the signs of bad tenancy in [6 signs your off-campus landlord is exploitative](/blog/hostel-and-accommodation/exploitative-student-lodge-landlord/).

## 3. The 30-Day Tactical Relocation Playbook

If you decide to pack out, execute your exit with strategic precision:

* **Inspect in June/July, Not October:** Never wait until resumption week when thousands of freshers arrive in town and drive up lodge prices. Scout apartments during the final-year clearance window when prime rooms become vacant.
* **Secure Your Caution Deposit Early:** Request an official room inspection from your caretaker three weeks before departure to document that walls and plumbing are intact, ensuring your deposit is refunded.
* **Test Utilities at Night:** Before paying for the new room, visit at 8:30 PM. Verify that the compound gate is securely locked, the access street is illuminated, and the borehole pumps clean, odor-free water.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### When should you start scouting for a new lodge if you plan to move out?
Start inspecting new compounds 6 to 8 weeks before the end of the second semester. Graduating final-year students vacate early, giving you first access to prime rooms before the fresher rush.

### What is the average financial cost of moving between student lodges in Nigeria?
Moving costs roughly ₦80,000 to ₦140,000, including new agent and agreement fees (15-20%), new caution deposit, room repainting, and truck/tricycle moving logistics.

### What is the single biggest sign that you must move out of your current lodge?
Compromised security (repeated compound break-ins without landlord response) or permanent amenity collapse (borehole pump broken for over two months, forcing daily water hauling).

### Can a landlord arbitrarily increase student lodge rent by 50% upon renewal?
While state tenancy laws regulate arbitrary rent hikes, off-campus student towns operate with lax legal enforcement. Tenants must negotiate collectively or prepare to relocate.
