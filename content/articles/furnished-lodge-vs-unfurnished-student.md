# Furnished Lodge vs Empty Room: Buying Mattress & Rug

When inspecting off-campus student accommodation, caretakers frequently pitch **furnished student apartments**:

*"Just bring your clothes and laptop! Everything is already inside: double foam bed, executive reading desk, wardrobe, and window curtains."*

For freshers and busy students, moving into a ready-made room sounds effortlessly convenient. You avoid the chaos of visiting timber markets, negotiating mattress transport with motorcycle riders, and hammering nails into bare walls.

However, the economics and hygiene of pre-furnished student lodges rarely favor the tenant over a multi-year degree program. Here is the realistic cost, hygiene, and asset comparison.

---

## 1. The Sanitation Reality: Sleeping on Another Student's History

The most overlooked aspect of pre-furnished student lodges is **microbiological hygiene**:

* **The Bedbug Vector:** In university towns, bedbugs are an endemic residential plague. When an outgoing tenant vacates a furnished room after battling bedbugs for six months, the bugs do not leave with them. They nest deep inside the internal springs and foam core of the provided mattress.
* **Skin Allergies and Dander:** Shared mattresses accumulate hundreds of hours of bodily sweat, fungal spores, and skin dander from previous strangers.

Investing ₦65,000 to ₦85,000 in a brand-new, factory-sealed high-density mattress ensures you sleep in a sterile, healthy sanctuary throughout your academic journey.

## 2. The 4-Year Financial Calculus: Sunk Rent vs Tangible Equity

Let us examine the financial ledger across a standard 4-year Nigerian university degree:

### Option A: The Furnished Lodge Route
* **Annual Rental Premium:** ₦180,000 extra per session.
* **4-Year Cumulative Extra Rent Paid:** **₦720,000**.
* **Value of Furniture Owned on Graduation Day:** **₦0** (The landlord retains every piece).

### Option B: The Unfurnished Room + Personal Furniture Setup
* **Brand-New 6-Inch High-Density Foam:** ₦75,000
* **Locally Welded Steel/Wood Reading Desk & Chair:** ₦35,000
* **Quality Floor Rug & Blackout Curtains:** ₦25,000
* **Total One-Time Upfront Capital Outlay:** **₦135,000**.
* **4-Year Extra Rent Paid:** **₦0**.
* **Resale Salvage Value on Final-Year Clearance:** You can easily resell your mattress and desk to incoming freshers for **₦60,000**.
* **Effective Net 4-Year Cost:** **₦75,000** (versus ₦720,000 for furnished rent).

Purchasing your own essentials saves you nearly **₦650,000** over your undergraduate degree.

## 3. The Tenancy Damage Deposit Trap

Landlords love furnished lodges because every scratch becomes a pretext to forfeit your Caution Deposit:
* If the desk veneer peels from humidity, they deduct ₦25,000.
* If a curtain hook snaps, they bill you for complete replacement.
* When you own your furniture, you treat it with respect, rearrange it to suit your revision ergonomics, and leave the apartment cleanly without deposit deductions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the typical price premium for a furnished student lodge in Nigeria?
A furnished student room typically commands an extra ₦150,000 to ₦300,000 annually over an empty room in the same neighborhood, providing a bed, foam mattress, study table, plastic chair, and mini-wardrobe.

### What is the biggest hygiene hazard of pre-furnished student accommodations?
Second-hand foam mattresses. Previous occupants frequently leave behind deep sweat stains, fungal spores, and bedbug infestations embedded in foam cores that no superficial chemical spraying can eradicate.

### Is buying your own student furniture cheaper in the long run?
Yes. Buying a high-density 6-inch Mouka mattress, sturdy reading desk, chair, and floor rug costs roughly ₦120,000 to ₦160,000 upfront. This furniture serves you for all 4 to 5 university years and can be resold upon graduation for 40-50% salvage value.

### When does renting a furnished room make sense for an undergraduate?
For final-year transfer students, direct-entry students staying for only two sessions, or students from distant geopolitical zones who want to avoid the high logistics costs of transporting furniture.
