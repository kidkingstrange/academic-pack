# Final Year Clearance Hurdles: Avoiding Graduation Delays

You write your very last examination paper, step out of the faculty hall, and sign your name in black permanent marker across your coursemates' white graduation shirts.

You take celebratory photos in front of the faculty building, pop cheap champagne on the lawn, and hug friends you have spent four or five grueling years with. You believe the struggle is finally over.

Then, you step into the administrative administrative maze known as **Final Year Clearance**.

In Nigerian tertiary institutions, passing your final examinations is only half the battle. To receive your official statement of results, collect your degree certificate, and secure mobilization for the **National Youth Service Corps (NYSC)**, you must navigate an eight-station bureaucratic labyrinth:
1. Departmental Clearance (HOD & Project Supervisor)
2. Faculty Clearance (Dean of Faculty)
3. University Library Clearance
4. Bursary & Student Accounts Clearance
5. Student Affairs & Hall of Residence Clearance
6. University Health Services (Clinic) Clearance
7. Security & Directorate of Student Discipline Clearance
8. Alumni Relations & Senate Verification

Every year, thousands of Nigerian graduates miss their desired NYSC mobilization batch or spend six extra months stranded on campus because of a missing 100-level receipt, an unreturned library book from three sessions ago, or an unratified Continuous Assessment score.

Here is the exact step-by-step roadmap to conquer final year clearance without administrative bottlenecks and secure your prompt graduation.

---

## 1. The 4 Hidden Clearance Traps That Delay Graduation

Most clearance nightmares stem from administrative oversights that occurred years earlier:



* Missing 100L/200L Result] ➔ [Lost Bursary Payment Receipt] ➔ [Unreturned Library Book] ➔ [Senate Result Discrepancy



### Trap 1: The Missing Prerequisite Score
You passed GST 111 or MTH 102 in 100 Level, but during final transcript collation, the examinations officer discovers your score was omitted from the broad-sheet and recorded as "ABS" (Absent). Resolving a missing grade from three sessions ago requires tracing the original course lecturer, retrieving archived scripts, and filing an official Senate Result Amendment Form—a process that can take months.

### Trap 2: Lost Payment Receipts & Remita RRR Slips
The Bursary requires verified physical receipts for every single payment made throughout your academic career: acceptance fees, development levies, faculty dues, departmental levies, and course registration charges. If you lost your 100 Level bank teller or online receipt, you must pay re-issuance penalties or wait for manual ledger auditing.

### Trap 3: The Library "Ghost Book" Debt
In 200 Level, you borrowed a general chemistry or jurisprudence textbook for 14 days and forgot to return it. Two years later, the central library system flags your matriculation number for an overdue fine that has compounded into thousands of Naira.

## 2. Phase 1: The "Pre-Clearance" Document Dossier

Do not begin walking to administrative offices with loose sheets in your hands. Create a dedicated **Final Clearance Master Dossier**:

Purchase a sturdy, waterproof plastic document file folder containing at least **four clear photocopies** of each of the following:

| Document Required | Issuing Authority | Verification Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **JAMB Admission Letter** | JAMB Portal | Confirms statutory admission legitimacy |
| **All Semester Course Forms** | Departmental Portal | Proof of registered credit units |
| **All Session School Fee Receipts** | Bursary / Remita | Verifies zero outstanding financial debt |
| **Faculty & Departmental Dues** | Student Secretariat | Proves clearance with student associations |
| **Project Hard-Cover Deposit Slip** | University Library | Proof that final project copies were deposited |
| **Student ID Card** | Student Affairs | Returned for deactivation |

---

## 3. Phase 2: The Tactical Clearance Routing Protocol

Follow this exact sequence to minimize redundant walking across campus:



* 1. Library & Bursary Clearance] ➔ [2. Department & Faculty Clearance] ➔ [3. Student Affairs & Clinic] ➔ [4. Senate & NYSC Mobilization



### Step 1: Clear the Library First
Visit the central library early in the morning before long queues form. Present your library registration card. If you have overdue books, pay the statutory fine immediately and secure the official clearance stamp on your master clearance form.

### Step 2: Audit Your Bursary Ledger
Take all your payment receipts and Remita Retrieval Reference (RRR) receipts to the student accounts unit. Have the bursary auditing officer stamp your file confirming zero indebtedness.

### Step 3: Secure Departmental & Faculty Clearance
Submit your bound project copies to your supervisor and HOD. Obtain the HOD’s signature, then proceed to the Faculty Officer and Dean of Faculty.

### Step 4: Final Senate Ratification & NYSC Call-Up
Once your signed clearance dossier is submitted to the Academic Affairs Division, your name is entered into the **Senate-Approved Graduation List**. Once approved, your data is uploaded to the NYSC portal for mobilization.

## Final Year Clearance Action Checklist

Follow this systematic roadmap to graduate without administrative delays:

- [ ] **Assemble your Master Clearance Dossier:** Include 4 copies of all receipts, admission letters, and course registration forms.
- [ ] **Audit your transcript broad-sheet in Week 1:** Confirm that zero missing grades or calculation errors exist on your record.
- [ ] **Clear library debts early:** Return all overdue books and secure your library clearance stamp.
- [ ] **Deposit hard-cover project copies:** Secure signed submission receipts from your supervisor and department.
- [ ] **Verify your name on the Senate-Approved List:** Confirm spelling, date of birth, and JAMB registration number for NYSC call-up.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does final year clearance typically take in Nigerian universities?
Depending on institutional administrative speed and whether you have missing grades or library fees, final clearance takes between two and six weeks. Starting immediately after your final paper prevents missing NYSC mobilization batches.

### What causes the most common delays during university final clearance?
Missing continuous assessment scores from 100L/200L, unreturned departmental library books, outstanding school fee receipts from previous academic sessions, and delayed Senate result approvals.

### What is the green card or senate approved list?
The Senate-Approved List is the official document ratified by the University Senate confirming that a student has met all academic requirements for the award of a degree. Without Senate approval, you cannot be mobilized for NYSC.

### Can you begin final clearance before the university senate approves results?
Yes. You can complete informal clearance stages—such as returning library books, getting alumni signatures, and verifying bursary payment receipts—while awaiting formal Senate approval.
