# 5 High-Value Federal & State Bursaries Undergrads Ignore

Every academic session, state governments and federal intervention agencies allocate billions of naira for **Tertiary Student Bursaries and Educational Welfare Grants**.

Yet, when disbursement day arrives, only a tiny fraction of eligible students receive bank credit alerts.

Why?

Because the vast majority of students dismiss state bursaries as *"government politics that never pay,"* or they miss the narrow two-week registration window organized by student indigenous associations on campus.

While corporate scholarships demand a 4.0+ CGPA and brutal competitive aptitude tests, **State Bursaries are your statutory birthright as an indigene**:
* You do not need a First Class GPA to qualify.
* You do not need to write advanced numerical logic exams.
* You only need verified evidence that you are a registered student from that state or local government area.

Whether it is ₦25,000, ₦50,000, or ₦80,000, bursary funds can clear your semester hostel bills, buy essential handouts, or cover your food budget for two months.

Here are the **5 high-value state and statutory student bursary systems** you should never ignore, and how to claim your allocation.

---

## 1. The 5 Major Tertiary Student Bursary Streams

> ### Statutory Student Welfare Allocation Streams
>
> * 1. State Government Scholarship Boards (e.g., Delta State Bursary, Lagos State Bursary)
> * 2. Oil-Producing Area Development Commissions (NDDC, DESOPADEC, ASOPADEC)
> * 3. Federal Ministry of Education Statutory Bursaries for Education & STEM Majors
> * 4. Local Government Area (LGA) Chairman Educational Grants
> * 5. Community Town Union & Philanthropic Clan Foundations

### The Oil-Producing Commissions (NDDC / DESOPADEC)
If your university or home community is located within the Niger Delta or oil-bearing states (Delta, Rivers, Bayelsa, Akwa Ibom, Imo, Ondo, Abia, Edo), commissions provide substantial annual bursaries (ranging from ₦50,000 to ₦150,000) for registered student indigenes.

## 2. Navigating the National Association of State Students (NASS)

To receive state bursaries, you cannot operate in isolated secrecy on campus:

| Stage | Action Required | Critical Verification Entity |
|---|---|---|
| **Resumption Registration** | Register with your on-campus State Student Union (e.g., NAOS, NUBS) | Obtain stamped Indigenous Membership Card |
| **Bursary Portal Opening** | Submit accurate NIN, BVN, and matriculation number | Official State Scholarship Board Website |
| **Physical Screening** | Present original Certificate of Origin and Student ID | Visiting State Government Verification Delegation |

> **Crucial Warning:** Ensure your bank account name matches your Certificate of Origin and JAMB profile exactly. Discrepancies in middle names or spelling trigger automated electronic payment rejection.

## 3. Federal Education & STEM Bursary Allowances

Under federal educational intervention policies:
* **Federal Teachers & Education Major Bursary:** Students enrolled in Faculty of Education programs (B.Ed / B.Sc.Ed) in federal tertiary institutions are entitled to statutory annual stipends (up to ₦75,000) from the Federal Ministry of Education to encourage STEM teacher training.
* **PTDF Local Scholarship Scheme:** The Petroleum Technology Development Fund offers generous local scholarships for engineering, geology, and technology undergraduates.

## 4. Claim What Belongs to You

Do not let cynicism rob you of legitimate educational funding:
* Visit your university's Student Affairs Division once a semester to inspect the official notice boards for bursary announcements.
* Keep your Certificate of Local Government Origin scanned and ready on your phone.
* Claim your statutory allowance, reinvest the funds into your academic materials, and power your journey toward graduation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between an undergraduate scholarship and a state bursary?
Scholarships are merit-based competitive awards requiring high CGPAs (3.50+), while state bursaries are statutory welfare entitlements granted to all verified indigenes of specific states or local government areas enrolled in tertiary institutions.

### Which Nigerian states pay consistent annual bursaries to their tertiary students?
States with active, funded scholarship boards include Delta, Lagos, Bayelsa, Kano, Rivers, Oyo, and Edo State, with annual payments ranging from ₦20,000 to ₦100,000 per student.

### What documents are required to claim state bursary disbursements?
Certificate of Local Government Origin, current semester stamped course registration form, student university ID card, and National Association of [State] Students (NASS) membership verification receipt.

### Why do many eligible students fail to receive their state bursary funds?
Failure to participate in mandatory on-campus physical verification exercises organized by National Association of State Students executives, or entering inaccurate bank account details on state bursary portals.
