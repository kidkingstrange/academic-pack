Continuing Unwanted Degree vs Starting Fresh with JAMB

How Nigerian students can decide whether to endure an unwanted university course (like Botany or Agriculture) or rewrite JAMB to pursue Medicine or Law.

Continuing Unwanted Degree vs Starting Fresh with JAMB

You sit in an agricultural science or polymer engineering lecture hall.

For three years in secondary school, your singular dream was to study Medicine and Surgery or Law.

You wore scrubs in your dreams. Your parents introduced you to relatives as "our future doctor."

Then JAMB results arrived. You scored 248. The university cutoff for Medicine was 285.

To avoid staying at home for another year, you accepted the university's compromise transfer offer: Fisheries & Aquaculture or Soil Science.

Now, you are in 200 Level.

Every single day feels like intellectual torture:
* You have zero passion for the coursework.
* Your CGPA is sliding into 2.30 because you cannot bring yourself to read handouts you hate.
* You see secondary school coursemates in medical school on social media, and your heart aches with regret.

You face a terrifying choice:

Option A: The Fresh Start: Buy another JAMB UTME form, rewrite the examination, and fight for your dream course from 100 Level all over again.

Option B: The Sunk Cost Push: Swallow your pride, endure the remaining three years, graduate with this unwanted certificate, and pivot your career afterward.

Here is the pragmatic, data-driven analysis to make this monumental life choice.

1. The Sunk Cost Fallacy vs. The 4-Year Trap

In behavioral economics, the Sunk Cost Fallacy leads people to continue an unfulfilling endeavor simply because they have already invested time and money:

Already Spent 2 Years In Botany

  • Mindset Trap: "I cannot waste those 2 years; I must suffer for 2 more years"
  • Result: Graduating with a Third Class / 2.2 in a field you will never practice

On the flip side, the Grass-Is-Greener Trap leads students to underestimate the brutal unpredictability of Nigerian university admissions:
* JAMB cutoffs shift erratically every session.
* ASUU strikes and institutional quotas mean you could spend two additional years sitting at home without securing your dream course.

2. The Decision Scorecard: Rewrite vs. Finish

3. The Third Option: Direct Entry and Postgraduate Pivoting

Many Nigerian students mistakenly believe that your undergraduate major locks you into a lifelong career cage:

  • The Direct Entry (DE) Route: If you finish a 3-year or 4-year degree with a First Class or Second Class Upper, you can apply for Direct Entry admission into 200-level Medicine or Law without writing UTME.
  • The Modern Corporate Reality: In corporate Nigeria, top management consulting firms (McKinsey, KPMG, PwC), commercial banks, and technology startups hire graduates of any discipline who demonstrate exceptional problem-solving, analytical aptitude, and communication skills.
  • Master's Conversion Programs: You can transition from a science degree into an MBA, Data Science, or Corporate Finance through postgraduate conversion degrees abroad or locally.

Review semester deferral options in deferring a semester vs staying enrolled on a bad GPA.

4. How to Execute the Safe "Parallel Track"

If you decide to rewrite JAMB, never drop out of your current university first:

  1. Register for JAMB UTME as an external candidate while maintaining your normal student profile.
  2. Prepare for UTME quietly in evening study blocks. Do not broadcast your plan to roommates or lecturers.
  3. Write the examination and monitor the admission list.
  4. Only when your new admission is officially confirmed on JAMB CAPS and school fees are ready do you formally withdraw from your previous institution.

Never leap into the dark without a safety net underneath you. Make your move with calculated precision.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should a 200-level student rewrite JAMB if they were admitted into an unwanted course?

If you have a clear, realistic pathway to your desired professional course (such as scoring 300+ in JAMB) and your mental health is in total collapse, rewriting is viable. However, if you are already in 300 level, finishing your first degree with a First Class and pursuing a direct-entry second degree is usually far safer.

Can you change department within your university without rewriting JAMB?

Yes. Most Nigerian universities offer intra-faculty and inter-faculty transfers at the end of 100 level, provided you achieve a minimum CGPA (usually 3.50 or 4.00) and obtain HOD approvals.

What happens if you rewrite JAMB while still registered as an undergraduate?

JAMB regulations allow registered students to take UTME. However, you should not deregister or abandon your current degree until an official new admission letter is printed and verified on the JAMB CAPS portal.

Does graduating with an unwanted degree ruin your career prospects in Nigeria?

No. In modern banking, fintech, tech ecosystems, and consulting, employers care about analytical reasoning, leadership, and technical digital skills rather than whether your certificate says 'Zoology' or 'Biochemistry'.
