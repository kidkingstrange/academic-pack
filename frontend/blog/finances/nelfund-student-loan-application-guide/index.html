NELFUND Student Loan: Eligibility & Application Walkthrough

For decades, Nigerian undergraduates faced a brutal economic reality: if your parents could not afford university school fees, you were forced to drop out or defer indefinitely.

In 2024, the Federal Government established the Nigerian Education Loan Fund (NELFUND) under the Access to Higher Education Act.

For the first time in modern Nigerian history:
* Public university undergraduates can access interest-free loans covering 100% of approved institutional school fees.
* Eligible students receive a direct monthly upkeep allowance of ₦20,000 paid into their bank accounts.
* Repayment does not begin until two full years after completing NYSC.

Yet, across university campuses, hundreds of thousands of deserving students have not applied due to confusion, misinformation, or intimidating portal errors.

Here is the authoritative, step-by-step application walkthrough to successfully secure your NELFUND tuition and upkeep allocation.

1. Eligibility Criteria & Verification Prerequisites

Before opening the portal, ensure you meet the statutory eligibility requirements:

Eligibility Pillar Verification Requirement Common Rejection Reason
Pillar 1: Identity Synchronization Full legal name, date of birth, and phone number match 100% across NIN and BVN Name arrangement discrepancies (e.g., surname and middle name swapped)
Pillar 2: JAMB CAPS Admission Official JAMB Admission Letter uploaded on JAMB Central Admissions Processing System Institutional admission not yet ratified on JAMB portal
Pillar 3: Senate List Upload Verified matriculation number present on the institution's official Senate List sent to NELFUND Department has not completed data reconciliation with the central student affairs portal
  1. Identity Alignment: The full name, date of birth, and phone number on your NIN (National Identity Management Commission) must match your BVN (Bank Verification Number) and JAMB profile with zero typographical errors.
  2. Institutional Accreditation: You must be enrolled in an accredited Federal or State tertiary institution that has officially uploaded its student data to the NELFUND verification dashboard.

Execute your application via the official NELFUND student portal (nelfund.gov.ng):

3. Financial Profiling Enter your BVN and verified personal commercial bank account Account name must match NIN and university records
4. Loan Type Selection Check boxes for Institutional Fees AND Monthly Upkeep Allowance Both can be selected simultaneously
5. Document Upload Upload clear PDF of JAMB admission letter and student ID File size must be under 500KB

3. Understanding the Repayment Architecture

Many students fear applying because of predatory loan horror stories:

  • Zero Interest Rate: NELFUND is strictly an interest-free statutory facility. You do not pay compound interest, application fees, or hidden administrative charges.
  • The 2-Year Post-NYSC Grace Window: You are legally protected from repayment until twenty-four months after passing out from NYSC.
  • The Automated 10% Deduction: Once employed, your employer deducts 10% of your monthly salary at source toward the Fund. If you are self-employed or unemployed, you submit an affidavit without harassment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible to apply for the NELFUND student loan in Nigeria?

All registered undergraduate students in recognized federal and state universities, polytechnics, and colleges of education across Nigeria who possess a verified JAMB admission letter, valid NIN, and BVN.

Does the NELFUND student loan cover living stipends or only school tuition?

NELFUND covers both: 100% of approved institutional school fees (paid directly to the university's account) plus a monthly upkeep allowance (currently ₦20,000/month) paid directly into the student's personal bank account.

What documents are required to complete the NELFUND portal application?

JAMB admission letter, National Identification Number (NIN), Bank Verification Number (BVN), university matriculation number, and current semester course registration form.

When does repayment of the NELFUND student loan begin?

Repayment commences two years after completing the mandatory National Youth Service Corps (NYSC) program, through a 10% deduction from the graduate's monthly salary or business earnings.
