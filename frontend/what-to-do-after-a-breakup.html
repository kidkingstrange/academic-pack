THE GRIEF & SHOCK OF HEARTBREAK

Are you living through this quiet torment?

Heartbreak is not just an emotion; it is an acute neurochemical withdrawal that shakes your entire identity.

You wake up with a sinking heaviness in your chest, reaching for your phone only to remember they are no longer there.
You catch yourself compulsively checking their WhatsApp "last seen", Instagram stories, or following their friends' posts.
You replay every past conversation in your head, torturing yourself wondering what you said wrong or could have done differently.
You feel like you've lost your identity—wondering who you even are now that you are no longer half of a couple.
You battle unprovoked anxiety, wondering if they are already with someone else or laughing without you.
You feel terrified that you will never find someone who understands you that way, or that you have ruined your future.
